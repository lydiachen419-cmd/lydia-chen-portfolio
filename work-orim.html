Data Automation & Revenue Forecasting

Situation

Monthly revenue projections required separate Tableau exports, manual deal counts, and repeated transfers across worksheets.

Task

Automate data collection, preparation, and forecasting so the team could plan promotions faster.

Action

Connected Tableau and Google Sheets with Python scripts for KDD, Goldbox, KBD, KMD, BookBub, US deal counts, and month-to-date sales. Integrated processing and forecasting scripts into an AI-triggered Slack workflow with results delivered to the team channel.

Result

Reduced processing time from 3 days to 20 minutes; delivered four data-sync script types and a user guide to support ongoing forecast refreshes and resource planning.
