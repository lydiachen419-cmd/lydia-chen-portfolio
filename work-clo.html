CLO's Brand Communications team hadn't had a dedicated data or analytics role before this internship — most content decisions were made on instinct. Over three months I built three systems that gave the team its first real feedback loop: what content works, why, and how to scale the parts that do.

One

2026.0 Launch & Creator Strategy

  • GTM Strategy
  • Creator Partnership
  • Cross-functional Coordination

Situation

A release with multiple new tools needed feature-specific stories for different creator niches.

Task

Develop feature-level content plans and coordinate product accuracy, storyboards, and creator fit.

Action

Aligned with Product on 7+ core features, helped plan video storyboards, and matched specialist creators to product demonstrations.

Result

A lead feature video reached 590K+ accounts and generated 54K+ engagements.

Two

Content Diagnostics & Attribution

  • Data Analysis
  • Dashboarding

Situation

The team had no dedicated analytics role and relied on manual checks of content performance.

Task

Build a repeatable feedback loop connecting content, spend, and marketing decisions.

Action

Built a UTM tracker, standardized campaign links, integrated TikTok and Instagram data through APIs, and developed an AI-assisted content diagnostic workflow.

Result

Delivered monthly recommendations for planning and review. The dashboard covered 480+ posts across 8 categories and identified Reels as 72% of the high-performing tier.

Three

AI Tagging & Visual Asset Operations

  • AI Automation
  • Ops Systems

Situation

More than 2,000 creator assets were scattered across Drive and difficult to retrieve.

Task

Make the library searchable and usable in campaign planning.

Action

Combined ffmpeg preprocessing with visual-LLM classification by garment type, complexity, color, and aesthetic, validated a pilot, and connected the tags to a search interface.

Result

Delivered a library with multidimensional filtering so the team could find assets by campaign direction. No standardized time-saving measurement was recorded.
