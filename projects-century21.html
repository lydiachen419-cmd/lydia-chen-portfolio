Century 21 NYC — Gen Z Brand Repositioning

Strategic Consumer Insight

Team of 5Hanlan Cai, Zixin Meng, Sierra Bao, Zhihan Hu, Xiuhan Chen

  • Brand Positioning
  • GTM Strategy
  • Consumer Research
  • GEO/AEO

Situation

Gen Z impressions of Century 21 were shaped by outdated secondhand signals rather than store visits.

Task

As a core researcher, turn consumer insight into a brand-introduction and store-visit strategy.

Action

Used 8 in-depth interviews and 7 focus groups to identify the perception gap; developed a style-first, price-second KOL brief, UGC activation, and GEO/AEO and O2O recommendations.

Result

Delivered the “Good taste. Better price.” positioning and a plan linking awareness, creator content, and store visits. This was a research deliverable; store-traffic uplift was not measured.
