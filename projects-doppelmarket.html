Doppel Market — Digital Twin Adoption Research

Market research and growth strategy

Team of 5Sierra Bao, Zixin Meng, Sarah Cho, Xiuhan Chen, Leon Tang

  • Market Research
  • UX Research
  • Growth Strategy

Doppel Market builds "digital twins" — AI proxies that can answer research surveys on a person's behalf. The question we were asked: are financially motivated, lower-socioeconomic individuals a viable early-adopter segment for this?

We ran this in two phases — 5 in-person qualitative interviews (frontline/service workers) and a 28-item Qualtrics survey (n=21).

The core finding, and the one that reframed the whole project: financial compensation is the #1 reason people participate in research generally — but it's the weakest driver of whether they'll actually adopt a digital twin specifically. Digging into why people hesitated, the answer wasn't price. It was trust: unauthorized use of their twin, data leaks, and simply not understanding how the thing works were the top three concerns, well ahead of any complaint about pay.

Digital-twin adoption isn't a financial problem — it's a trust problem.

We mapped a data-sensitivity hierarchy and built a six-part recommendation set split into "Building Trust" (visible trust moments, monthly impact summaries, community-anchor partnerships) and "Building Value" (time-saved messaging, instant micro-rewards, referral credits). We also flagged our own study's real limitations directly, including a sampling skew (52% Asian respondents vs. ~7.3% national average).
