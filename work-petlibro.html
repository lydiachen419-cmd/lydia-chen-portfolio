I identified, segmented, and onboarded 60+ pet-tech and lifestyle creators, screened by content vertical, audience quality, and platform fit — then ran every partnership through the same loop: brief, produce, publish, review, iterate. Two creator examples show the range this covered: a straightforward product-education video (urinary blockage awareness tied to the Dockstream fountain) that hit 56.8K likes, and a paid-partnership lifestyle integration that pulled 3,395 likes and drove direct traffic to the product page.

To make that repeatable at 60-creator scale, I built two systems: standardized shooting briefs — scene-by-scene timing, visual direction, must-shoot feature call-outs — so creators had a clear structure without losing their own voice (this lifted creator response rates roughly 30%); and a 500+ creator tracking database and outreach template, covering multiple product lines, that the whole marketing team ended up adopting — cutting outreach-to-confirmation turnaround by 40%.
