Style Muse — AI Wardrobe & Styling App

Solo project

Intro to User Experience

  • UX Research
  • Product Design
  • AI Prototyping

Situation

Style-conscious women had inspiration, wardrobes, and shopping tools in separate places, making occasion-specific outfit decisions difficult.

Task

Design a more connected decision experience for women aged 20–40 with clear styling needs.

Action

Used interviews and journey mapping to locate friction; developed wireframes, high-fidelity Figma screens, and Interview Ready tags, then iterated a Lovable prototype for styling, search, wardrobe management, and sharing.

Result

Delivered the full research-to-interactive-prototype flow. The demo is available online; production user or conversion outcomes have not been measured.
